Progress

The native <progress>: a track and the done part. Without a value it is busy, and a light travels along it; with motion reduced, it stands still.

Spinner

<progress class="spinner">: the same element as a ring, for something with no end to show.

Meter

The native <meter>: a measure in a known range. low, high and optimum sort the value into the best part, the middle and the worst.
